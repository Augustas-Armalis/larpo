# larpo context

last consolidated: 23 august 2026

## the company

larpo is a full-stack web partner for saas and ai teams. it takes a company from an early idea or an existing, underpowered brand to a clear identity and a custom website that is live, connected and ready to grow.

the commercial promise is simple: one senior team handles the whole website, from the first strategic decisions through the final technical connection. clients do not need to coordinate separate brand, web-design, development and launch partners.

larpo is not a product design studio. the focus is the brand and marketing website around a product: how the company is understood, trusted and chosen.

**the underlying buyer truth**

a company has reached a more serious moment than its current identity or website can support. it might be a launch, funding round, repositioning, enterprise push or shift from founder-led sales to a repeatable marketing motion. larpo makes the company look as credible and clear as the business has become.

## audience

primary buyers are founders and small growth teams at seed to series b saas, ai, fintech and software companies. they are usually moving quickly and need a visible step up in market perception without building an internal brand, design and engineering function.

buyer triggers:

- a launch, fundraise or major product release
- a promising product with an early-stage or inconsistent public presence
- a website that does not explain a complex offer clearly enough
- more serious buyers, partners or enterprise conversations
- fragmented work across freelancers, templates and separate agencies

## what larpo does

- cms, analytics, integrations, domain and deployment setup

## differentiation

larpo should lead with ownership and completion, not generic agency language. the meaningful difference is that the work does not stop at mockups or a handoff: larpo defines the message, creates the brand, designs the site, writes the custom code, connects the important systems and helps get it live.

do say:

- one team from first idea to live website
- strategy, identity, web design and custom code in one place
- a clear brand and website for the next stage of the company
- custom built and ready to launch

avoid:

- product design studio
- world-class, premium, innovative, immersive, full-service
- vague claims about digital experiences or transformative design
- unsupported performance promises, invented case-study outcomes or made-up client endorsements

## verbal identity

the voice is direct, calm, compact and product-literate. it is confident without acting like an agency brochure. it explains the practical outcome first, then the craft behind it.

- use lower case for public site copy unless a proper name or platform convention calls for capitals
- use short sentences and specific nouns
- say what is built, who it is for and what happens next
- put proof close to the claim
- keep calls to action plain: `book a call` and `write us a message`
- never use em dashes in larpo copy

example: `a complete brand and custom website, for bigger teams and projects`

not: `we craft transformational digital experiences for visionary founders`

## visual direction

larpo is clean, monochrome and restrained: closer to mobbin's editorial discipline and the compact commercial clarity of avero and kargul than a typical animated agency site.

- mark only in the navigation; mark-and-wordmark lockup in the footer or formal contexts
- black, white and neutral gray only; no yellow-gray cast, gradients, blobs or decorative glow
- geist is the only typeface, with restrained negative letter spacing
- small, rounded, borderless surfaces where grouping needs help
- generous but controlled spacing; work and proof carry more visual weight than oversized headlines
- secondary actions use a light neutral background with no border
- use lucide icons consistently and sparingly
- buttons never jump upward on hover
- a thin black branded scrollbar is acceptable

## site experience

the homepage sells through immediate clarity, visual proof and a simple path to contact:

1. hero with current-project availability, direct claim, short supporting copy and two ctas
2. an infinite, clickable work reel leading to `/work/`
3. trusted-by logos and a `20+` neutral proof tile
4. four concise capability cards
5. compact two-up testimonials with square portraits and controls that advance by two
6. clear starting-price formats with both ctas
7. concise faq, first item open, question underline on hover
8. quiet footer with large invisible social hit areas, no telegram

the work archive is a visual browse page: a large body of selected landing-page references displayed as an easy, two-column desktop and one-column mobile scroll. its job is to demonstrate taste and scale, not to read like a dense case-study database.

motion should feel premium but disappear into use: short, smooth opacity and small vertical entrance transitions with a gentle custom ease. reveal elements late enough to be noticed but early enough to avoid empty space. respect reduced-motion preferences. do not use blur-fly-in effects or decorative scroll effects.

## references and research synthesis

the visual and commercial references are ingredients, not templates:

- **mobbin:** quiet navigation, tight spacing, work as the main visual proof
- **avero:** obvious formats, pricing clarity, paired book/message ctas and compact social proof
- **kargul:** short positioning, clean founder/studio social bios and restrained presentation
- **kree8:** memorable personality and social presence, moderated for larpo's quieter system
- **lander, deserve, parallel, pixeldarts, artone, lumi, skyloom, victorflow, we optimizz, gemeos and framebite:** direct creative-studio positioning, work-led storytelling and concise service framing
- **designjoy, halaska, trueform, crafted, daasign, futured and hydro:** useful offer and pricing comparisons for the seed-to-series-b software buyer

the august 2026 x research shows that strong studio content is led by real visual proof, short process clips, useful micro-tools or teardowns, opinionated craft posts and occasional capacity signals. the founder account should do more of the posting; studios are often quieter.
