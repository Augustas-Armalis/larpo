# Larpo Studio brand foundation

## Positioning

Larpo is a senior-led website design and development studio for SaaS, AI, fintech, and software teams with a launch or growth milestone ahead.

**Primary line:** Website design and development for SaaS & AI startups.

**Plain-language descriptor:** Strategy, identity, website design, and development for funded SaaS, AI, and fintech teams.

**Primary audience:** Seed to Series B software founders and small growth teams that need stronger market perception without adding a full internal design function.

**The category difference:** One senior team owns the entire website—from the first logo and page structure through design, development, and launch.

## The strategic choice

The studio market is full of interchangeable promises: “design partner,” “world-class digital experiences,” “fast and flexible,” and “from idea to launch.” Larpo should not compete by stacking more agency adjectives onto the same claim.

The useful whitespace is **the next-stage moment**. Larpo is for a software company whose business has become more credible than its current presentation. That makes the buyer, trigger, and outcome visible in one sentence—and gives the portfolio a point of view beyond visual taste.

The commercial story follows one line:

1. Your company has reached a higher-stakes milestone.
2. The identity, message, and website need to tell the same story.
3. A senior team can make that change without the usual agency layers.
4. Start with the smallest format that can move the milestone forward.

## Reference synthesis

- [Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/) contributes disciplined spacing, quiet navigation, and work presented as the product rather than as decoration.
- [Kargul Studio](https://kargul.studio/) contributes restraint, compact proof, and a direct call/message CTA pair.
- [Avero Studio](https://avero.studio/) contributes commercial clarity: visible formats, starting prices, and the existing booking and message routes.
- [Kree8](https://www.kree8.studio/) contributes personality and confidence, but Larpo stays quieter and more product-literate.
- [Parallel](https://www.parallelhq.com/) and [Deserve](https://deserve.studio/) reinforce the value of strong typography, a limited palette, and proof close to the claim.

These references are ingredients, not templates. Larpo's aperture mark, milestone positioning, black-and-white restraint, and connected brand/web/product offer create the ownable combination.

## Buyer triggers

- A funding announcement or new round
- A product launch that has outgrown its early-stage identity
- Moving from founder-led sales toward a repeatable marketing motion
- Entering enterprise conversations where trust and clarity matter more
- A fragmented product, brand, and website built by separate contributors

## Offer architecture

### Launch sprint

One conversion-led landing page, copy direction, custom visual language, responsive design, and live Framer build. Typical range: from €4k, 2–3 weeks.

### Marketing site

Positioning workshop, brand refinement, multi-page website, motion system, Framer or React development, and CMS handoff. Typical range: from €9k, 4–6 weeks.

### Website support

New pages, conversion improvements, visual assets, and ongoing design and development after launch. Flexible scope.

## Verbal identity

Larpo should sound direct, calm, specific, and product-literate. Use short sentences. Prefer the actual outcome over agency adjectives.

Use: "A launch-ready website in three weeks."

Avoid: "We craft immersive digital experiences that elevate visionary brands."

Use proof near the claim. Say who the work is for. Name the milestone. Avoid "world-class," "premium," "innovative," and "full-service" unless the evidence is on the same screen.

## Visual system

- **Paper:** `#FFFFFF`
- **Ink:** `#151513`
- **Soft surface:** `#F5F5F2`
- **Quiet:** `#73736D`
- **Line:** `#E9E9E6`

The exact Figma aperture mark is the signature. The navigation uses only the mark; the compact mark-and-name lockup is reserved for the footer. Do not scatter decorative versions throughout the page.

Geist is the only typeface. Body copy uses `-0.015em` tracking, buttons stay close to `-0.01em`, and display lines tighten to roughly `-0.045em`. Scale stays restrained: work, spacing, and proof carry the hierarchy instead of oversized headlines.

The page order is fixed: direct hero, infinite work reel, selected clients, concise website capabilities, compact testimonials, pricing, FAQ, and footer. Surfaces are borderless wherever grouping remains obvious through spacing or a subtle neutral fill.

## Motion principles

- Hero content and work enter once with opacity and small vertical transforms.
- Scroll reveals use opacity and small vertical transforms only.
- Image hover movement stays below 2% scale; button movement stays within 1px.
- The work reel may loop continuously; it pauses on hover and becomes a static scrollable row when reduced motion is requested.
- Transparent surfaces are limited to the navigation, secondary actions, proof, and pricing. No blobs or decorative blur fields.
- Reduced-motion mode keeps all content visible and removes scroll-linked movement.
